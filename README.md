One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn procedurally by
`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) to `/output/entry.png`
(1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB). No network, no clock, no randomness:
two runs write byte-identical output.

## What is on the page

- Giant date: TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026, plus a KPI strip (Lahore AQI 500+,
  78 drones, 14 chess games, 557th Guru Nanak anniversary).
- Four hero stories: the World Chess Championship Game 1 in Geneva
  (Gukesh vs Sindarov), Iran's claim of 78 US drones downed, Lahore's
  AQI over 500, and India-China border talks in Arunachal.
- Eight more stories in a grid, a world map with pins, and a numbered
  index of six further items — 24 stories in total.
- Footer attestation: every story happened or was first reported on
  24 November 2026.

## Research

Fresh research at build time (00:00-00:20 UTC, 24 Nov 2026) from
chess.com / FIDE, Dunya News, Times of India / Indian Express,
ANI and Reuters wires. Stories from 21-23 Nov (COP31 collapse,
Indonesia factory fire, Dymer strike, India-Nepal train, India-NZ
Test draw, Ferrari launch) were deliberately left out: they are not
24 November news.

## Run

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
